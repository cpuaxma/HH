- **Version A, the original 33-second cut (1,980 frames).** The voiceover matches the text on screen word for word.
- **Version B, the current 43.5-second cut (2,610 frames).** The extra 1.5 seconds per scene give room for one more sentence each. Every scene still says its on-screen line, then adds a short line that's only spoken.

The current code renders version B. Version A is the commit before "hold every scene 1.5s longer" (`ecddd39`).

Times show when each scene is on screen. Scenes overlap by a sixth of a second, so a line can run slightly into the next scene.

### Text to read (B)


## Recording tips

- Record each scene as its own take, then drop it into your editor (CapCut, Premiere, etc.) at its start time from the table. That's easier than matching one long take to the cuts.
- Leave half a second of silence before and after each take so you can trim cleanly.
- Use a quiet room with soft surfaces, and hold the phone or mic about 15–20 cm from your mouth.
- The video has no audio. If you add music, keep it about 15–18 dB below your voice.
- The tashkeel (diacritics) is only there to guide pronunciation and emphasis. Read in natural Modern Standard Arabic.
- In version B, the extra sentences are spoken only; the on-screen text is unchanged. If you'd like captions for them too, the strings live in `src/strings.ts`.
