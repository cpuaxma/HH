**Voiceover script for `traders-remotion` (40 s, 30 fps)**

- **Voice:** a calm, low and serious male or female voice in Modern Standard Arabic. Tense in the traps, warm and confident from «خطة واضحة» onwards.
- **Pace:** the traps are short punches. The four habits come fast, about one per 1.1 s, so read them as one flowing run with short breaths at the «...».
- **File:** put the recording in `public/voiceover.mp3`, starting at 0:00. The music then ducks under each line automatically.
- **Guide track:** `voiceover-guide-ar.srt` has the same lines with exact in and out times. Import it into your editor or TTS tool as a timing guide.

## Source note for the 75% figure

The on-screen footnote reads: «تُظهر إفصاحات الوسطاء أن نحو ٧٥٪ من حسابات الأفراد تخسر في تداول العقود مقابل الفروقات». CFD brokers regulated in the UK and EU must publish the percentage of retail accounts that lose money. Those disclosures typically fall around 70–80%. Check a current broker disclosure before posting. The per-trap numbers in the video are illustrative; only the overall share (three in four) reflects that figure.
