## Recording tips

- The script is about 65 words, roughly 27–30 seconds of speech at a normal pace. That fits in 33 seconds with short gaps between scenes.
- Record each scene as its own take, then drop it into your editor (CapCut, Premiere, etc.) at its start time from the table. That's easier than matching one long take to the cuts.
- Leave half a second of silence before and after each take so you can trim cleanly.
- Use a quiet room with soft surfaces, and hold the phone or mic about 15–20 cm from your mouth.
- The video has no audio. If you add music, keep it about 15–18 dB below your voice.
- The tashkeel (diacritics) is only there to guide pronunciation and emphasis. Read in natural Modern Standard Arabic.
