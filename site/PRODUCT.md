# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS, Motion, Lenis, React Three Fiber + drei. Chosen by the client. Static export, deployed to Vercel from the `site/` folder.

## Users

Business owners in Lebanon (cafes, shops, clinics, restaurants, service firms, any trade) who know their social media is not working and want someone to run it. Most arrive on a phone from an Instagram or TikTok link, curious but not yet committed.

## Product Purpose

Marketing site for Hot Pepper Media, a social media marketing agency in Beirut serving every type of business. Success is a booked call.

## Positioning

The "heat" metaphor is the brand: services are sold as heat levels on a Scoville scale, from a mild start to full heat.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services: Strategy, Content production, Community management, Paid ads, Reporting.
- Process: Taste, Recipe, Cook, Turn it up.
- Languages on the page: English primary; Arabic and French appear in the marquee (Arabic must render right to left).
- No real client work, testimonials, statistics or prices on hand yet: every slot is a bracketed placeholder until the client supplies it.
- WhatsApp and Instagram handles: still to come.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Hot Pepper Media.
- Logo: `assets/logo-primary.png` (red wordmark, black outline chili, cream ground). `assets/logo-reversed.png` is a white stand-in for dark grounds, to be replaced by the official reversed file.
- Brand red from the logo, about #E23B24.
- Tagline for the site: "Social media, served hot."

None yet. Testimonials get their own section near the end, filled with placeholders.
