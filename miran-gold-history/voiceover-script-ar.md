# نص التعليق الصوتي (عربي): تاريخ الذهب في ٦٠ ثانية

The Arabic voiceover for `out/gold-history-ar.mp4` (60 seconds), written for an AI voice.

**Every line starts with the scene's headline (the big text), word for word**, so the voice matches what the viewer is reading. Numbers and years are written as words. The video is dense, so the longer version only adds a short follow-on where a scene has room. The small follow-ons repeat the smaller line on screen.

Two beats are timed to the voice:
- **The 1971 chain** snaps on «انفصل».
- **The chart** draws from 1971 to 2026 while line 9 is spoken.

The outro screen is in English, so line 12 is in English too. After it, the logo screen holds until 1:00.0 for outro music.

## Making it fit exactly

- **Make each line its own clip** and drop it at its "Place at" time. Each clip starts as its headline appears.
- **Check each clip's length against "Max length".** If a clip runs over, either:
  1. Set that clip's speed to 1.05–1.1.
  2. In Version B, drop the bold follow-on. The headline alone always fits.
- **The year lines are the fullest** (5, 6, 8), because spoken years are long. Check those first.

## Keeping it natural (not "robotic Arabic")

- **Spoken forms:** years and numbers are written as words, and «م» is said as «للميلاد». There is no «…», «:», «$» or Latin text. Commas and full stops make the pauses.
- **Light tashkeel only.** Diacritics are added only where the AI could misread a word:
  - «الذَّهَب» is marked so it isn't read as «الذَّهاب» (going).
  - «حُليّ» is marked so it is read as jewellery.
  - «سُكّت» and «سُكّ» are marked so they are read as "were minted".
  - «حمّى» carries a shadda.
- **Voice:** use a native Arabic voice, the same one as the gold and silver reels. Stability around 40–50%, style low, speed 1.0.
- **Music:** keep it about 15–18 dB below the voice while it's speaking. In the last 1.5 seconds it can come up.
