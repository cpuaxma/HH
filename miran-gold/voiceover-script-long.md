## Full script (one paste)

Three mistakes are quietly wiping out beginner trading accounts. Are you making any of them?

Mistake one: trading with no stop loss. One bad trade can wipe out weeks of hard work. Set your exit before you enter, so every loss stays small.

Mistake two: risking too much on a single trade. Keep each trade to around one to two percent of your account. That way, no single loss can end the game.

Mistake three: trading on emotion. Revenge trades and chasing the market turn small losses into big ones. Plan your entry, stop loss and target, then stick to it.

Protect your capital first. Profits second. For more, join the Miran Gold community. Link in bio.
