## Heartbeat (`sfx/heartbeat.mp3`)

One short "lub-dub" (under 0.4 s). It is triggered on **every beat of the HUD heart-rate counter**, so the sound speeds up and slows down exactly with the on-screen number, louder when the pulse races. That is more precise than one looped file with a fixed rate: Remotion cannot change an audio file's speed over time.
