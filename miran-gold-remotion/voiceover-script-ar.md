لماذا يرتفعُ الذهب، عندما ينخفضُ الدولار؟

الذهبُ مُسعَّرٌ بالدولار الأمريكي.

## Tips

- The script is about 50 words. At a normal Arabic pace that comes to roughly 25–28 seconds of speech, which leaves gaps for the visuals and music.
- If a line runs long, generate each scene as its own clip and place it at its start time. That's easier than speeding up one long take.
- The diacritics (tashkeel) on some words are there to guide the voice generator's pronunciation. Keep them in the text you paste.
